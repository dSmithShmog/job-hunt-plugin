# voice.md template

Structure for the user's voice file. Fill from real samples first
(see voice-extraction.md); interview only for gaps. Keep headings verbatim —
the other skills look for them.

```markdown
# Voice

Derived from: <list the samples read, or "interview only — lean on examples/ as it grows">

## Register

<!-- 2-4 sentences describing how this user writes: sentence length,
     formality, contractions, humor, how direct they are. -->

## Signature moves

<!-- Constructions that recur across their samples and that they've approved
     reusing: characteristic openers, closers, framings. Quote them verbatim
     with a note on where each works. -->

## Approved lines

<!-- Exact sentences the user has approved for reuse across applications
     (identity lines, closers). Verbatim only. -->

## Banned and flagged

<!-- Words, punctuation, and constructions this user rejects. Include both
     universal cliches they hate and personal quirks. Anything here gets
     killed in self-review before a draft is shown. -->

## Review corpses

<!-- Lines that died in past reviews, quoted, with WHY they died. The
     cheapest way to stop the same mistake twice. Append every time the user
     kills a line. -->

## Formatting rules

<!-- Punctuation and mechanics: dash policy, contraction policy, greeting
     and sign-off conventions, letter length target. -->

## Outreach

<!-- Register for cold messages (LinkedIn notes, emails). Defaults to the
     job-contact skill's no-ask register; record here any user-approved
     phrasing that overrides it, plus their identity line and closer. -->
```
